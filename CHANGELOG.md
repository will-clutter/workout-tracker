## 2026-11-09

### Changed

- **Number boxes only take positive numbers.** Sets, reps, heart rate, calories and the hours / minutes / seconds boxes take whole numbers. Weight and distance also take decimals, like 2.5 or 0.5. You can't type a minus sign, a plus sign, an "e" or letters, and pasting something like "-12abc" keeps just the number. Zero isn't allowed either, but you can still type "0." on the way to "0.5". An empty box is always fine, and Notes still take anything. Your cursor stays where it was when a wrong key is ignored.
- **Minutes and seconds stop at 59.** In the cardio Duration box you can no longer type 60 or more in the minutes or seconds parts; the key is simply ignored (typing 6 then 0 stays at 6). Hours still take any number, so 90 minutes is entered as 1 hr 30 min. Durations you already saved aren't affected.
- **The Distance box fills the row.** On a cardio card it used to sit at half width with a gap beside it; it now stretches across.
- Numbers that were already saved as negative (or as nonsense) are cleaned up the next time the app opens, on your workout, your cardio list and in History.

### Fixed

- **One damaged History entry could wipe all your saved data.** If a single History entry was broken, the app threw away everything and started fresh. It now skips or repairs the broken entry and keeps the rest.
- A cardio duration that was infinite or nonsense could show up as "Infinityh". It now shows nothing, and very large durations are capped at 999,999 hours.
- Odd saved data (a workout with no exercise list, an exercise with no muscle list) no longer causes errors in search or when logging.

### Behind the scenes

- **Added 66 more tests** (139 in total), in a new file `tests/edgecases.test.js`, that try to break things on purpose: negative, zero, huge and nonsense numbers, damaged saved data, strange search text, and the typo-suggestion limits. Also removed one older test ("90 minutes reads as 1h 30m") that checked something you can no longer type.

## 2026-11-08

### Added

- **Cardio tab.** A new tab with a heart icon, between Workout and History. Tap **Add Cardio** to pick from the 14 cardio exercises (treadmill, bikes, elliptical, rower, Stairmaster and more), or create your own (like Swimming or Hiking). Each one has boxes for duration, heart rate, calories and distance, a Low / Medium / High intensity choice, and notes. You only see what you fill in: an entry with just a duration shows "25 min", and one with nothing filled in shows just its name. Cardio works like your workout: swipe left to cross off, swipe right to bring back, swipe a choice right to add it quickly, and crossing everything off logs it to History with a "Cardio complete" glow.
- **"Did you mean…?" in the exercise search.** If a search finds nothing because of a typo, it says, for example, *No results for "bciep curl"* with a **Show results for "bicep curl"?** link underneath. It also catches words typed without a space, like "benchpress". If nothing close is found, you see the usual "No matches" message.

### Changed

- **History is split into Workouts and Cardio.** A switch at the top of History shows one or the other. Sessions you logged before today are all under Workouts.
- **A friendlier empty screen.** When your workout or cardio list has nothing in it, a quiet "Nothing here yet!" box shows where the cards would be. It doesn't appear when you've simply crossed everything off.
- **Cardio exercises no longer show up in the Workout tab's search**, since they now have their own tab.
- The bottom bar is a little wider to fit the third tab.
- **Cardio duration takes hours, minutes and seconds.** The Duration box now has three parts (hr : min : sec), and you can leave any of them blank. It reads in words and skips the parts you didn't use: "25 min", "25m 30s", "1h 5m 30s". Typing 90 in minutes reads as "1h 30m". Cardio you already entered in whole minutes is kept and shows the same as before.

### Fixed

- In cardio, a duration, heart rate, calories or distance set to **0** showed up on the card and in History. Zero is now treated as "not filled in" and isn't shown.

### Behind the scenes

- **Added 8 more tests** (74 in total) for hiding zeros, the new duration format, and changing cardio saved in minutes to the new format.

- **Added 22 more tests** (66 in total) for the new cardio entries (what's shown when only some fields are filled in, how they're saved to History, and how data saved before the Cardio tab still loads) and for the typo suggestions.
- Crossing off, removing and adding now share the same code for workouts and cardio, so both tabs behave the same way.

- **Added the first automated tests.** Ten tests check the small helpers the app relies on: making counts like "3 exercises", making typed text safe to show on screen, capitalizing names, setting up a fresh workout, and adding an exercise with 3 sets × 10 reps. Run them with `npm test` (needs Node.js).
- Moved those helpers out of `js/app.js` into a new `js/logic.js` file so the tests can load them. The app works the same as before.
- Added `CLAUDE.md` with notes on how the tests are set up.
- **Added 18 more tests** for how the app handles your data: reading saved data (and starting fresh if it's missing or broken), finding the workout on screen, finding an exercise, saving a session to History with what was and wasn't done, and un-crossing exercises from an earlier day. The logic behind these moved into `js/logic.js`, and the app works the same.
- **Added 16 more tests** for the exercise search (search words, muscle and equipment filters, and all of them together) and for the small body maps on exercise cards (whether they show the front or back, and whether they zoom in on the upper body, the legs or the whole body). That brings the total to 44.
- **Cardio exercises are marked again.** The 14 cardio exercises in the database (treadmill, bikes, elliptical, rower, Stairmaster, step mill, rope jumping, skating, trail running, prowler sprint) are tagged as cardio again, to get ready for a cardio tab. Nothing in the app looks different yet. Three new tests check the tag, including one that fails if it's ever removed by accident.

## 2026-11-07

### Added

- **Swipe right to add an exercise.** In the exercise search, drag a result to the right, past halfway, to add it to your workout without opening it. The search stays open, so you can add several in a row. An exercise that's already in your workout snaps back with a short message. Tapping a result still opens its details, as before.
- **Swipe left to cross off an exercise.** In your workout, drag an exercise to the left to mark it done. A line draws through its name, the card dims, and it moves down into a new **Completed** section. Drag a completed exercise to the right to bring it back.
- **Finishing a workout logs it automatically.** When every exercise is crossed off, the session is saved to History and the screen glows with a "Workout complete" message. Finishing the same workout again later that day logs another session.
- **Crossed-off exercises reset.** Use the **Reset** button next to "Completed", or come back on a later day and they reset on their own.
- **History shows what you finished.** If you log a session with the button before crossing everything off, the exercises you skipped are dimmed and marked "Not done", and the count reads like "3 of 4 exercises done".
- **First-time swipe hints.** The first time you see a workout card or a search result, it slides partway open by itself and back, to show that it can be swiped. Each hint only plays once.
- **Sliding tab indicator.** A glowing circle moves between the Workout and History tabs when you switch.
- **The search header gets out of the way.** In the exercise search, the "Add Exercise" title and close button slide away while you scroll down the results, and come back as soon as you scroll up.

### Changed

- **Search shows 10 results at a time**, down from 50. "Show more" adds 10 more each time.
- **The app starts faster.** Exercise instructions moved into their own file, which is only downloaded the first time you open an exercise's details. The file the app loads at startup went from 850 KB to 162 KB.
- **The heading font is now part of the app** instead of coming from Google Fonts, so the page no longer waits on Google before it can appear.
- **Emojis are replaced with icons.** The color theme, note and delete buttons now use the same style of icons as the rest of the app.
- **Theme colors are defined in one place** (`css/style.css`), so adding or changing a theme only means editing that file plus one line in `js/app.js`.

### Fixed

- In the exercise search, results scrolled over the top of the panel's header, which made the close button hard to tap.
- The phone's status bar now matches your color theme right away when the app opens, instead of briefly showing the wrong color.
- The color theme button in the theme panel did nothing when tapped. It was a duplicate of the one in the top bar and has been removed.
- Mini body maps on exercise cards now pick the front or back view correctly for neck exercises that also work back muscles.

### Behind the scenes

- Removed unused and repeated code from the stylesheet, page and scripts, and made the body diagrams quicker to draw.
- Removed unused fields from the exercise database.
- Credited the Cinzel font in the README and included its license (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
- Removed the outdated screenshots.
